Case Study Information

Step-by-step walkthroughs of real algorithmic problems and their solutions.

Two Sum

Problem: Given an array of integers and a target, find indices of two numbers that add up to the target.

Solution: Use a hash map to store seen values and their indices for O(n) lookup.

Complexity: Time O(n), Space O(n)

Valid Parentheses

Problem: Determine if a string of brackets is valid (properly opened and closed).

Solution: Use a stack to match opening and closing brackets in order.

Complexity: Time O(n), Space O(n)

Maximum Subarray

Problem: Find the contiguous subarray with the largest sum.

Solution: Kadane's Algorithm — track the running sum and reset when it goes negative.

Complexity: Time O(n), Space O(1)
